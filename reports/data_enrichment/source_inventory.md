# Sources actually checked, 26 October 2026

The [machine catalog](../../data_sources/catalog.yaml) records 82 resource/source entries with independent discovered, accessible, downloaded, parsed, integrated and validated stages. A null stage means it was not established; metadata access is distinguished from acquisition of observations. Exact URLs, checksums, sizes, dates, variables, units, licenses and restrictions are recorded per resource. Mechanical validation does not establish forecast skill.

NWDP declares Other (Open) without a specific license URL; raw observations stay local with CGWB/NWIC attribution. IMD pages request scientific citations without an inspected open redistribution grant. CGWB reproduction requires attribution and excludes third-party material. ERA5 metadata reports CC-BY-4.0; each scientific auxiliary resource retains its own terms. See [groundwater details](groundwater_inventory.md), [weather details](weather_sources.md) and [auxiliary details](auxiliary_sources.md).

The original raw source's 52,304 location fingerprints and 1,470,970 distinct station-days become 58,527 and 1,627,123 after union with accepted NWDP rows. These are conservative matching aids, not certified physical well counts. The baseline already has a separate January 2026 refresh: recovering older missing years does not make all current observations fresher.

Downloaded raw files, station-level audits and generated model inputs remain ignored. Source manifests and compact aggregate coverage are committed. The [reproduction guide](../../docs/OFFICIAL_DATA_ENRICHMENT.md) separates explicit acquisition, offline audit/features, paired evaluation and isolated publication; none runs during a dashboard request.
