# AquaIQ scientific validation and reliability audit — 2026-10-18

## A. Executive decision and baseline

**Recommendation: continue research-only shadow evaluation.** Retain the existing active artifacts while collecting evidence; their continued presence does not establish operational validity. Do not promote HGB or reselect a model from these test results. No new P0 defect was confirmed in the reviewed paths. Five release-blocking evidence gaps remain:

1. Historical publication, receipt and revision timestamps do not establish that all features were available at forecast issuance.
2. Observations are sparse and stale; the September 2026 feature grid does not contain September groundwater measurements.
3. District means do not track a certified fixed well panel; changing composition and repeated aggregates affect evaluation.
4. Current-period serving estimates groundwater anchors, whereas the saved monthly holdout evaluated observed anchors. Prospective skill remains unestablished.
5. The crisis index has no independent outcome calibration. ANN agreement with fuzzy labels cannot supply that calibration.

The repository has substantial safeguards: exact calendar targets, explicit observed-label flags, bounded causal filling, training-only learned preprocessing, paired baselines, hashed model bundles, transactional scoring, unavailable-result states, and an isolated append-only shadow ledger. These support reproducibility and disciplined experimentation; they do not establish accurate nationwide forecasts, calibrated crisis probabilities, causal policy effects, or production readiness.
