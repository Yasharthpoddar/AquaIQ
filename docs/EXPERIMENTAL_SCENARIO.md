# Experimental twelve-month monthly scenario

The user approved a separate experimental scenario on 21 October 2026 after the current-data and direct-M+12 limitations were explained, then requested coverage across all districts. This does not change saved ML models, source data, historical evaluation or the real shadow campaign. HGB remains abstained.

The application defaults to the next twelve full calendar months after the current Asia/Kolkata date. For 21 October 2026, these are November 2026–October 2027. A full-month series does not predict today's daily measurement or guarantee twelve months of lead to a future acquisition day.

The file-only adapter retains Adilabad's existing frozen research bundle and numeric scenario. Other canonical districts use their own history in the existing `data/processed/features_matrix.csv`, verified against its manifest and a separate pinned configuration, `research/experimental_scenario_sources.json`. The existing canonical crosswalk supplies IDs/display metadata; the source export and registry must have exactly the same identities. The new lock records source, manifest and registry hashes; revisions fail closed and require a separately reviewed version. Parsing is cached only after all source bytes have been reverified. No DB fallback, guessed ID, zone aggregation or cross-district substitution is used.

The method uses source-marked observed groundwater monthly means, never imputed rows, and excludes current-month and later rows. National source units follow the repository's `m_bgl` convention; that convention does not independently certify a per-well datum. Acquisition, first availability, well count and panel identities are absent from the national export and remain unknown/null. The separate Adilabad source retains only its already reported well-count/panel information. Calendar filtering is not proof of point-in-time availability. Past `as_of` requests remain current-vintage scenarios.

The adapter reuses `models/monthly_projection.py`'s existing robust seasonal/trend calculation: at least 24 observations spanning 36 months; recent 120-month window where enough observations exist; age weights with a 60-month half-life; four robust fitting iterations; penalized linear trend and annual sine/cosine when estimable; recent residual level adjustment; trend damped with a 36-month time scale; depths floored at zero. This transient NumPy fit is calculated in memory and never saved. No six-month estimator is extrapolated, no M+12 point is interpolated into a monthly curve, and no active ML is trained, tuned, loaded or promoted.

The same method estimates today's monthly level across the gap from the last source-marked observation and then calculates each of the next twelve months directly from the fitted seasonal/trend function. It assumes historical behavior continues. Rainfall, temperature, evapotranspiration, pumping, interventions and future weather are excluded; this is not the climate-conditioned six-month product. Sparse or changing source panels can bias the scenario. Phase coverage and fit counts are returned rather than presumed; unsampled calendar phases are inferred by the assumed seasonal function and are not observed monthly measurements.

Current canonical evidence has 36,890 observed-marked rows across 652 districts; 73 have no observations, and imputed values are excluded. The unchanged method supports 548 districts, with 104 insufficient histories and 73 empty histories explicitly unavailable. Most eligible districts have January 2026 as their latest source month; nine have January 2021, requiring a 69-calendar-month gap estimate at October 2026. Adilabad retains its separate May 2026 source. In the national eligibility audit, 500 eligible districts lack 24 recent observations, so the existing method uses its full-history branch rather than its recent 120-month window. Many have only four sampled calendar phases. These long gaps and sparse sampling limit credibility; twelve numerical points do not establish nationwide accuracy or independently validated horizons.

Responses and UI disclose source-marked observation depth/month, estimated scenario-month depth, calendar age, contributing well count, fit dates/count/phase coverage, computation time, source/method/configuration hashes and unknown availability. The source hashes identify retained bytes, not a verified historical release chain. No calibrated uncertainty intervals, skill metrics or prospective issuance records are generated. Unsupported districts and insufficient history abstain without fallback.

Use `/api/research/scenario/HR-Kaithal?months=12` or `/district/HR-Kaithal?view=scenario#forecast-graph`, or select any district on the existing map/list. All 725 IDs have a twelve-month scenario view; eligible districts get twelve own-history estimates, and others get a reasoned unavailable state. District graph controls retain the historical M+12 view with its original Adilabad scope, and explicit `origin_month` links continue to use it. An API `as_of=YYYY-MM-DD` is accepted for explicit scenario-date inspection; the main UI requests today's date by default. The historical risk score still uses its preserved historical model; it is independent and is not converted to a validated twelve-month score by the scenario.

Release boundary: a local, labelled academic demonstration only. Establishing forecast skill requires a preregistered method, verified current source availability and well/datum continuity, genuinely future labels, and comparisons to fixed persistence/seasonal baselines. The scenario must not be presented as verified real-time forecasting or promoted as an active model on the strength of this graph.
